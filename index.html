About the App

Tap Tap Friend empowers young adults of all abilities with a friendly digital companion. Built on Glide and ChatGPT, this app helps our crew express themselves, connect with others, and lead their day with confidence — all with the gentle guidance of Jelly the Jellyfish!

Key Features

Morning Chat Café

Daily check-ins using emojis and kind reflections to support emotional wellbeing.

Ask Jelly!

Our friendly AI helper answers questions with love, clarity, and encouragement.

Clubs for All

Join Cooking, Art, Music, Sports, and more—each one tailored for all abilities to explore and enjoy.

Jelly Board

A digital wall where the crew can post artwork, photos, kind notes, and life updates.

Want to Learn More?

Tap Tap Friend is available to explore by request! If you're part of an inclusive community, a support worker, or just someone curious about accessible tech, we'd love to connect with you.
